FEATURE DESCRIPTIONS
================================================================================

FEATURE 1: 🛡️ FRAUD DETECTION
Purpose: Identify suspicious or fraudulent property listings
How It Works:
  - Detects suspicious keywords (URGENT SALE, CHEAP DEAL, NO BROKER, etc.)
  - Identifies price anomalies (>40% below market average)
  - Finds duplicate titles (>80% similarity match)
Output:
  - Trust Score (0-100): Higher is more trustworthy
  - Risk Level: Low/Medium/High
  - Flags: List of issues found
Accuracy: ✅ Validated against test cases


FEATURE 2: 💰 WHY THIS PRICE?
Purpose: Explain the reasoning behind property pricing
How It Works:
  - Analyzes location appeal
  - Considers property size/area
  - Evaluates amenities & furnishing
  - Assesses condition & age
  - Accounts for market demand
Output:
  - Main reason for price point
  - Key factors with descriptions
  - Impact assessment (positive/negative/neutral)
Accuracy: ✅ Based on property attributes


FEATURE 3: 🏘️ NEIGHBORHOOD REPORT
Purpose: Analyze area livability and quality
How It Works:
  - Calculates livability score (0-100)
  - Evaluates safety rating
  - Identifies nearby facilities
  - Provides neighborhood summary
Output:
  - Livability Score (0-100)
  - 18 nearby facilities (schools, hospitals, shops, transport, etc.)
  - Neighborhood quality assessment
Accuracy: ✅ 18 mock facilities, expandable with real data


FEATURE 4: 📊 MARKET INSIGHTS
Purpose: Provide market trend analysis & investment guidance
How It Works:
  - Analyzes price trends (up/down/stable)
  - Calculates demand levels (high/medium/low)
  - Shows YoY growth rates
  - Rates investment potential
Output:
  - Price Trend indicator
  - Demand Level
  - Growth Rate (percentage)
  - Investment Rating (Strong/Moderate/Caution)
Accuracy: ✅ Data for 10 Indian cities


FEATURE 5: ⭐ RECOMMENDATIONS
Purpose: Find similar properties matching user criteria
How It Works:
  - Accepts user preferences (budget, bedrooms, location)
  - Finds similar properties
  - Calculates multi-factor match scores
  - Ranks by relevance
Output:
  - Top 3-5 recommendations
  - Match Score (0-100%) per property
  - Reason for each recommendation
Accuracy: ✅ Based on multi-factor scoring
